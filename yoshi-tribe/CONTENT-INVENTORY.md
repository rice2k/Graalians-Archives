## Historical context represented in the files

The recovered news documents Yoshi Tribe activity through 2001–2002, including the August 2001 end of the official guild, later discussion of revival, and a May 2002 Dino Valley/Yoshi site update. The timeline also indexes later surviving web captures, so a capture date should not automatically be interpreted as the date the underlying community content was originally written.

## Preservation rule

Recovered content should be retained as recovered. Archive-created navigation, indexes, explanations and reconstructed presentation should be labeled as such. Missing historical files should remain identified as missing rather than replaced with invented substitutes.

## Public/private rule

This inventory is safe documentation for the public repository. Files containing credentials, private account information, or non-public personal data should not be committed. Historical public-web material still receives a privacy review before migration.
